The global burden

The need for cancer care is still enormous.

More people are surviving cancer, but outcomes remain far worse when the disease has already spread at diagnosis. As the number of people diagnosed grows, so does the need for more effective treatments.

The immunotherapy market

The global cancer immunotherapy market is projected to reach $306B by 2033.

Cancer immunotherapy includes checkpoint inhibitors, cell therapies, and vaccines designed to treat cancer. Grand View Research projects the broader category will grow by about 9% a year from 2026 to 2033.

Source National Cancer Institute

The treatment gap

Survival is improving. Advanced cancer remains harder to treat.

U.S. five-year relative survival reached 70% across all cancer diagnoses. For people whose cancer had already spread to distant parts of the body at diagnosis, it was 35%.

Where CancerVax fits

CancerVax is developing a universal cancer treatment platform designed to help the immune system recognize cancer cells. The goal is to develop off-the-shelf injections that can be adapted for different cancer types without manufacturing a separate treatment for each patient.
